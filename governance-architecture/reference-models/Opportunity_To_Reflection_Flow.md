# Opportunity to Reflection Flow (v1.0.0)

This is the standard reference model for the Service Economy OS (Phanda).

## 1. Opportunity (A+)
- A customer submits a request.
- *Legality Lattice* ensures the customer's identity is verified via iPhande before creating the opportunity.

## 2. Lead (A)
- Opportunity transitions to a qualified Lead.
- Assigned to an Organization (Tenant).

## 3. Quote (A)
- Technician generates Quote.

## 4. Invoice & Payment (A)
- Quote accepted -> Invoice generated.
- Payment triggers -> Work unblocks.

## 5. Work (A)
- Technician completes physical labor.

## 6. Reflection (B)
- Semantic note attached to the completed work.

## Traceability (C+)
The causal chain is verified: Reflection -> Work -> Payment -> Invoice -> Quote -> Lead -> Opportunity.
