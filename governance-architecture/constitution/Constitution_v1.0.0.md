# The Phanda Operating System Constitution (v1.0.0)

## Preamble
**Ownership and Stewardship**
The **Phanda Operating System**, including its architecture, constitutional framework, governance model, and all associated intellectual property, is owned by **Global IT and Business Solutions**.

**Gift Nkosinathi Chisali** is recognized as the **Founder and Constitutional Architect** of the Phanda Operating System and its governing epistemic framework.

All constitutional articles, amendments, governance processes, and future evolution of the platform operate under this stewardship unless lawfully transferred or amended through the organization's authorized governance processes.

This preamble establishes ownership and stewardship only. It grants no additional execution authority to any system layer and does not alter the constitutional separation of responsibilities defined within the operating system.

---

## Article I — Separation of Worlds
- Higher worlds observe lower worlds.
- Lower worlds never depend on higher worlds.

## Article II — Meaning is Non-Executable
- Interpretation cannot execute reality. World B cannot mutate World A.

## Article III — Truth Preservation
- Historical records are append-only. No silent rewriting.

## Article IV — Human Sovereignty
- Human arbitration is final. The system cannot invent authority.

## Article V — Simulation is Non-Binding
- Simulations are advisory. Predictions never become execution.

## Article VI — Constitution Above Evolution
- Constitutional articles override evolutionary proposals. Amendments follow a formal process.

## Article VII — Ownership & Stewardship
- Global IT and Business Solutions is the owner of the operating system and associated intellectual property.
- Gift Nkosinathi Chisali is recognized as Founder and Constitutional Architect.

## Article VIII — Transparency
- Every decision made by Worlds A–F must be explainable through traceable evidence.

## Article IX — Accountability
- Every override, amendment, and constitutional exception must be permanently recorded with a timestamp, actor, rationale, and evidence.

## Article X — Trust Before Automation
- When certainty falls below the defined constitutional threshold, the system must defer to human governance rather than speculate or execute autonomously.
