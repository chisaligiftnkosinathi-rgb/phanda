# The Human Loops of Phanda

Phanda is **not** a job board. It is **a continuity economy**.

People don't come because they want jobs. People come because they have a need, and someone else has the ability to meet that need. Phanda simply connects the two and preserves trust.

The human loop is actually:

```
Need
  ↓
Discovery
  ↓
Connection
  ↓
Agreement
  ↓
Work
  ↓
Evidence
  ↓
Trust
  ↓
Continuity
  ↓
Opportunity Again
```

The relationship is the product. The job is only one step.

---

## The Core Entities

Our domain model is constructed around six core entities that generate everything else:

1. **Need** - A problem that requires solving.
2. **Ability** - The verified skill to solve a problem.
3. **Opportunity** - The intersection of Need + Ability.
4. **Evidence** - Proof of work performed.
5. **Trust** - Reputation built from Evidence, History, Continuity, Verification, and Recommendations. (Trust is much harder to fake than simple 5-star ratings).
6. **Continuity** - The ongoing relationship and repeating cycles of opportunity.

---

## The Two Public Intentions

The two primary tabs in the public layer are not "Jobs" and "Profiles." They represent fundamentally different human intentions.

### 1. Find Work
**Question answered:** *"Who currently needs me?"*

Instead of browsing abstract listings, this answers the immediate need: "Show me people who need my ability nearby."

**The Lifecycle:**
1. I have a skill.
2. Show me people needing it nearby.
3. I express interest.
4. We agree.
5. I perform.
6. Evidence is recorded.
7. My reputation grows.
8. Better opportunities appear.

### 2. Find People
**Question answered:** *"Who can help me?"*

Instead of searching static profiles, this connects a problem to a capable person.

**The Lifecycle:**
1. I have a problem.
2. I describe it.
3. Phanda identifies capable people.
4. I compare trust.
5. I choose someone.
6. I receive work.
7. I leave evidence.
8. The relationship continues.

## Progressive Capability (Freemium Model)

No separate app is needed. Capabilities are unlocked progressively based on identity and trust.

- **Public**
  - Browse, search, and view basic profiles.
- **Registered**
  - Express interest, create opportunities, and build continuity history.
- **Verified Steward**
  - Unlock evidence ledger, identity verification, payment proof, and reputation growth.
- **Professional**
  - Access ActionStream, analytics, business dashboard, automation, and premium visibility.
