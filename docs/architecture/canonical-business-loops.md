# Canonical Business Loops: The Laws of the Ekasi Economy

This document defines the fundamental, irreducible economic and social loops that exist in the township economy (*ekasi*). These loops are the laws of the ecosystem. They are true regardless of whether digital technology exists; the role of the platform is simply to preserve, digitize, and scale their memory so that value can accumulate instead of resetting to zero.

---

## The Core Philosophy

> **iPhande is a system for preserving economic memory so that visibility, trust, opportunity, continuity, and grace can accumulate instead of resetting to zero.**

Every feature, endpoint, and operational process in the system must serve one or more of these core laws. If a concept does not strengthen these loops, it does not belong in the core ecosystem.

---

## The 6 Canonical Loops

### Loop 1: The Visibility Loop
Opportunity cannot flow to what cannot be seen. 

```text
Person Exists
    ↓
Person Becomes Visible
    ↓
Community Becomes Aware
    ↓
Opportunity Can Find Them
    ↓
More Visibility
```

* **The Law:** Invisible capability cannot participate in the economy.
* **Platform Manifestation:** Steward onboarding, public profiles, and localized spatial registries.

---

### Loop 2: The Opportunity Loop
Value creation occurs at the intersection of visible need and discovered capability.

```text
Need Emerges
    ↓
Need Becomes Visible
    ↓
Capability Is Discovered
    ↓
Connection Is Formed
    ↓
Work Begins
```

* **The Law:** Opportunity is created when visible need meets visible capability.
* **Platform Manifestation:** Geographic match feeds, smart routing systems, and lead-claim pipelines.

---

### Loop 3: The Evidence Loop
Work that leaves no trace cannot be remembered. Without a record, the economic value of effort decays instantly.

```text
Work Performed
    ↓
Evidence Created
    ↓
Evidence Preserved
    ↓
Memory Strengthened
    ↓
Future Confidence Increased
```

* **The Law:** Unrecorded work eventually disappears.
* **Platform Manifestation:** Receipts, photo evidence, invoice archives, and inventory/stock consumption tracking.

---

### Loop 4: The Trust Loop
Trust is not a static score; it is a compilation of remembered reality.

```text
Evidence Accumulates
    ↓
Reputation Forms
    ↓
Trust Increases
    ↓
Larger Opportunities Arrive
    ↓
More Evidence Generated
```

* **The Law:** Trust is remembered evidence over time.
* **Platform Manifestation:** The trust engine, community annotations, follow-up logs, and verification states.

---

### Loop 5: The Continuity Loop
Resilience is not the absence of storms; it is the capacity to weather them.

```text
Disruption Occurs
    ↓
Impact Observed
    ↓
Memory Preserved
    ↓
Adaptation Occurs
    ↓
Activity Continues
```

* **The Law:** Continuity is not the absence of disruption. Continuity is the ability to continue despite disruption.
* **Platform Manifestation:** Continuity events, status captures, network impact graphs, and the telemetry "River".

---

### Loop 6: The Grace Loop
In a highly volatile economic environment, survival is a collective responsibility.

```text
Struggle Appears
    ↓
Community Notices
    ↓
Support Arrives
    ↓
Recovery Happens
    ↓
Future Steward Continues
```

* **The Law:** Communities survive by carrying one another through disruption.
* **Platform Manifestation:** Peer-to-peer giving ledger, solidarity campaigns, and scripture/resilience reflections.
