# Human Journeys

Phanda is not a job board or a social network. It is an **evidence-driven trust marketplace**. 
People do not come to Phanda to browse. They come to solve a problem. Every screen should move them one step closer to solving it.

Every API, every screen, and every database table exists to support the continuous progression of trust through these foundational loops.

---

## Journey 1: Solving a Problem (Finding People)

**The Goal:** "I need someone I can trust to solve my problem."

1. **Visitor** arrives with a need.
2. **Intent Declared:** "I need help."
3. **Category Selected:** e.g., Plumbing, Electrical, Cleaning.
4. **Location Declared:** "Nearby".
5. **Matching:** Discovers verified people.
6. **Evidence Evaluation:** Views the `EvidenceRecord` (past outcomes, verification, trust score).
7. **Identity Established:** Creates an account to initiate contact (Progressive Authentication).
8. **Conversation:** Discusses the problem.
9. **Agreement & Execution:** Work is completed.
10. **Verification:** Customer leaves confirmation of the outcome.
11. **Trust Grows:** The worker's trust score increases.
12. **Continuity:** The relationship is preserved for tomorrow's opportunity.

---

## Journey 2: Providing a Solution (Finding Work)

**The Goal:** "I have an ability and want to use it to earn income."

1. **Visitor** arrives with a skill.
2. **Intent Declared:** "I want to work."
3. **Category Selected:** Chooses their primary ability.
4. **Location Declared:** "Nearby".
5. **Matching:** Discovers local opportunities requiring that ability.
6. **Identity Established:** Creates an account to apply.
7. **Acceptance:** Worker accepts the opportunity.
8. **Evidence Captured (Before):** Captures the initial state (optional depending on category).
9. **Execution:** Work is performed.
10. **Evidence Captured (After):** Uploads outcome proof, documents, or timestamps.
11. **Verification:** Customer verifies the evidence.
12. **Trust Grows:** Evidence becomes a permanent part of their `EvidenceRecord`.
13. **Continuity:** Elevated trust leads to higher visibility for future opportunities.

---

## The Core Design Philosophy

- **One screen. One decision.** 
- **Browse anonymously. Participate intentionally. Verify when trust matters.**
- **Lead with the problem, not the money.**
- **Trust isn't claimed. It's demonstrated through evidence.**
